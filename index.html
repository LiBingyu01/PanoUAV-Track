About the benchmark

PanoUAV-Track is a benchmark for full-spherical UAV visual tracking, comprising 1,895 sequences, 507,961 frames and 2,872 annotated objects across real-world daylight, nighttime and simulation. We also introduce PUTrack, a geometry-aware tracking framework for latitude-dependent distortion and ERP seam discontinuity.
